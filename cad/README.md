Revision A is preserved as the source of the retained, modified Open Duck mechanism. B replaces its enclosure and electronics packaging, corrects the battery allowance, and adds serviceable lighting and switch/fuse mounts. Use B's manifest and instructions for this build.

The model is a digital prototype based on published dimensions and stated adjustable capacities. Read the verification record before selecting parts or commanding motion; physical fitting and powered performance have not been tested.
