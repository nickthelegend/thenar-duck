# 03 — Feetech STS3215 bus servo: mechanical + electrical reference (for CAD and BOM)

Research date: 2026-09-26. Scope: the only actuator allowed in the microduck (BDX-style biped, PETG on a Bambu P1S, ≤ INR 50,000 total). Every number below carries a source URL; numbers I could not verify are marked **[unverified]** or given as my own estimate with the assumptions spelled out.

How the geometry was obtained: Waveshare publishes a DXF + PDF of the ST3215 body (Waveshare's ST3215 *is* the Feetech STS3215). I downloaded the zip, parsed the DXF `CIRCLE`/`DIMENSION` entities and rendered the PDF, and cross-checked against the outline drawing on page 6 of Feetech's own ST-3215-C001 and ST-3215-C018 datasheets.

## 1. Mechanical

### 1.1 Outline (units mm)

Datasheet nominal: **45.2 × 24.7 × 35** (Feetech C001 datasheet, item 6-1, [PDF p.4](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391772523943436695270694.pdf)); Waveshare's CAD drawing gives **45.22 × 24.72 × 35** ([ST3215-2D.zip](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/0/08/ST3215-2D.zip), sheet titled "SCS215" — the SCS215/STS3215 share one case). The Feetech drawing (datasheet p.6) shows 45.23 × 24.73.

Height stack (Waveshare side view + Feetech front view, both p.6 / drawing):

| Feature | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main case body (between the two flat mounting faces) | 29 | both drawings show "29" |
| + raised gear-head boss on the spline face | → 32 | Waveshare "32"; boss ≈ 3 mm high |
| + boss / rear cover on the idler face | → **35** | this is the catalogue "35" |
| Output spline protrusion above its face | 3.4 | Feetech drawing |
| Rear idler shaft protrusion below its face | 4.1 | Feetech drawing, shaft Ø6 |
| Overall spline tip → idler tip | 36.5 | Feetech drawing |
| Overall with both Waveshare aluminium discs fitted | 37.25 | Waveshare drawing |

Caveat: the two drawings don't agree exactly on how 29/32/35/36.5/37.25 nest; for the CAD reference model use the STEP file, not these numbers (see §1.6).

### 1.2 Output shaft and the raised gear-head boss

- Shaft axis is **10.11 mm from the gearbox end face** and **on the width centreline** (12.36 from each side). Waveshare DXF: dimension `10.111704` in both face views; circle centres at x = 93.796 with body edge at x = 83.684.
- The output and rear idler shafts are coaxial ("double shaft", Feetech product name "双轴"; [Feetech C001 page](https://www.feetechrc.com/74v-19-kgcm-plastic-case-metal-tooth-magnetic-code-double-axis-ttl-series-steering-gear)).
- Raised D-shaped gear-head cover around the output: DXF arcs of r = 10.80–10.89 mm centred on the shaft axis (**≈ Ø21.6–21.8 round part**), flat edge **12.5 mm behind the shaft axis** (Feetech drawing "12.5"), ≈ 3 mm high (35 − 32). A printed part that sits on the spline face needs a ≥ Ø22 pocket or must stop 12.5 mm behind the axis.
- Spline: **25T, OD 5.9 mm** (Feetech item 6-9). DXF circles at the axis: Ø6.05 and Ø6.65 (spline tip/boss) and Ø2.6 (M3 tap drill for the **M3×6 rocker screw**, Feetech item 6-13).
- Rear idler shaft: **Ø6**, held with a **PA3.0×5 self-tapping screw** (Feetech drawing callout "PA3.0X5 的自攻螺丝"); DXF shows a Ø3.2 hole at the idler axis.

### 1.3 Mounting holes (there are no side flanges/ears)

The STS3215 has **no RC-style side ears**. It mounts through **8 threaded pilot holes, 4 on the spline face ("face A") and 4 on the idler face ("face B")**, for the supplied PA2.0 self-tapping screws (Feetech drawing: "8-PA2.0 自攻螺丝"). LeRobot's SO-101 instead drives **M2×6 machine screws** into these same holes, "two from the top and two from the bottom" ([LeRobot SO-101 assembly](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/en/so101)).

Coordinates from the Waveshare DXF (origin = gearbox end face × width centreline; x along the length, y across the width):

| Face | Hole pair | x from output end | y | Pilot Ø | Countersunk mouth Ø |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (spline) | near gear-head | **18.41** | **±10.25** | 1.6 | 2.0 |
| A (spline) | rear corners | **42.86** (= 2.36 from rear end) | ±10.25 | 1.6 | 2.0 |
| B (idler) | near gear-head | **18.41** | ±10.25 | 1.6 | 2.0 |
| B (idler) | rear | **39.11** (= 6.11 from rear end) | ±10.25 | 1.6 | 2.0 |

So the rectangles are **24.45 × 20.5 mm on face A** and **20.7 × 20.5 mm on face B** (DXF dimension entities `24.45`, `20.5`, `20.7`, `18.411704`, `6.111704`). The Feetech drawing independently shows the 6.11/18.41 pattern with plain holes and four cross-head case screws at the corners (Ø3.5 head recesses on face B at ≈ 3.0 mm from the output end and ≈ 2.4 mm from the rear end, 18.7 mm apart — DXF Ø3.5 circles at x = 126.984).

Interpretation caveat: the Feetech drawing labels its 6.11/18.41 view as the top view while the Waveshare DXF puts that pattern on the idler face. The DXF distinguishes the faces unambiguously (spline circles vs Ø3.2 screw hole), so I trust the DXF; **confirm on one physical unit before cutting the first mould/print**. Practical rule for CAD: the **18.41 mm pair exists on both faces** and is the safe pair; use the rear pair on whichever face you actually see the holes.

Design note for PETG: Ø1.6 pilot + PA2.0 self-tapper is Feetech's intent; for printed brackets, either drive M2×6 machine screws into the servo (SO-101 practice) or clamp the body between two printed halves and only use the horn and idler disc for load transfer (Open Duck practice — servo bodies are captured, horns bolted with M3).

### 1.5 Connector / cable exit

- Connector: **5264-3P (Molex 5264 family, 2.54 mm pitch)**, PP housing, **15 cm lead**, pins **1 GND / 2 VCC / 3 Signal-TTL** (Feetech datasheet item 6-7; Waveshare wiki "5264/2.54 3P"). Colour code on the supplied lead: black GND, red VCC, blue signal ([DFRobot](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-2961.html)).
- **Two sockets per servo** for daisy-chaining (Feetech drawing shows two "5264/2.54 3P 端子"; Waveshare wiki "two connectors per motor").
- Location: the Feetech rear-face view places the socket housings **≈ 12.5–16.5 mm behind the shaft axis** (≈ 22–26 mm from the output end) alongside a low block on the idler face that stands ≈ 1.5 mm proud between ≈ 5 and 17 mm from the rear end (Waveshare side view). The plugs come out sideways (parallel to the 24.7 mm faces). **[Exact socket position and plug clearance: verify on the STEP model / hardware — the two drawings are not explicit on this.]** Leave ≥ 10 mm side clearance for the plug + cable bend at the rear-bottom corner of each servo, or route a channel in the printed part.

Sources: [C001 datasheet](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391772523943436695270694.pdf) p.3–5; [C018 datasheet](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20260622/6391772519923113075854851.pdf) p.3–5; [Waveshare ST3215 product page](https://www.waveshare.com/st3215-servo.htm) (Kt, no-load current 200 mA/150 mA for the 12 V/7.4 V models); [Waveshare ST3215-HS page](https://www.waveshare.com/st3215-hs-servo-motor.htm).

C018 performance curve (datasheet p.6, 12 V): peak output ≈ 3.4 W at ≈ 15 kg·cm / ≈ 22 RPM / ≈ 1.4 A; peak efficiency ≈ 27 % at ≈ 6 kg·cm. Useful for the actuator model in sim.

Measured (Robonine, 12 V unit): static holding ≈ 35 kg·cm on a 10 cm lever; < 1 A under 1–1.5 kg dynamic load; 48 °C steady after 10 min holding 15 kg·cm; 60 °C after 50 min and 71 °C (overheat) after ~110 min of ±90° oscillation, with **no automatic thermal cut-off observed** ([Robonine](https://robonine.com/testing-of-feetech-sts3215-servomotor-backlash-repeatability-and-torque/)). Plan for thermal headroom in the knees.

**Battery warning.** The C001's datasheet range is 4–7.4 V, yet Open Duck Mini v2 runs it straight from a 2S Li-ion pack (Molicel P30B ×2, up to 8.4 V full) ([Open Duck BOM](https://orobot.io/o/program/BROKER-2/open-duck-mini)); Waveshare lists the 7.4 V model as "4–7.4 V" and OpenElab as "4–7.4 V" ([OpenElab](https://openelab.com/products/feetech-sts3215-c001-servo-7)). The over-voltage protection thresholds live in EEPROM registers 14/15 (max/min voltage, 0.1 V units — [register reference](https://github.com/commanderfun/STS3215/blob/main/REGISTER_REFERENCE.md)), so check the shipped default on your units. Cleaner options: (a) C018 on 3S (11.1 V nominal, 12.6 V full, inside 4–14 V) — this is what Waveshare/ThinkRobotics sell as the standard "12 V" model; (b) C001 on 2S behind a 7.2 V buck (Waveshare "DC Buck Adapter 7.2V", ₹334 ex-GST at [ElectronicsComp](https://www.electronicscomp.com/waveshare-serial-bus-servo-dc-buck-adapter-mini-module-design-for-serial-bus-servos-easy-to-use-72v-buck-regulator), current rating not published — **unverified for 14 servos**).

### 2.2 Encoder, resolution, feedback

- 12-bit magnetic angle sensor, 360° absolute, no mechanical limit, resolution 0.088° (360/4096), neutral 180° = 2048 (Feetech items 6-6, 7-6…7-8).
- Feedback fields: position, speed, load, input voltage, current, temperature (item 7-10). Present current register scale = 6.5 mA/LSB ([register reference](https://github.com/commanderfun/STS3215/blob/main/REGISTER_REFERENCE.md)).
- Max position update rate **1 ms** (item 11 "Max Position Update Rate 1ms").

### 2.3 Protocol, bus, connector, pinout

- **Half-duplex asynchronous TTL serial, 8 data bits, 1 stop, no parity**, "digital packet" commands (Feetech items 7-1/7-2). Same framing as Dynamixel Protocol 1.0 (0xFF 0xFF header, ID, length, instruction, params, checksum); STS/SMS send **low byte first** (Feetech FAQ item 11: "SCS series high byte first, SMS low byte first"; Waveshare demo `protocol_end = 0` for ST) — [Feetech start tutorial PDF](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20201127/start%20%20tutorial201015.pdf), [Waveshare user manual](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/f/f4/ST3215_Servo_User_Manual.pdf) p.25. Full spec: [Communication Protocol User Manual (191218)](https://files.waveshare.com/upload/2/27/Communication_Protocol_User_Manual-EN%28191218-0923%29.pdf).
- Signal levels: high 2–5 V, low 0–0.45 V (Feetech item 11) → 3.3 V hosts are fine.
- Baud: 38,400 – 1,000,000 bps, factory 1 Mbps (item 7-4). Baud register codes 0…7 = 1 M, 500 k, 250 k, 128 k, 115200, 76800, 57600, 38400 ([Feetech tutorial p.4](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20201127/start%20%20tutorial201015.pdf)). (LeRobot's table lists 19200 instead of 76800 for the last code — [tables.py](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/main/src/lerobot/motors/feetech/tables.py); trust Feetech.)
- IDs 0–253 (254 = broadcast); Waveshare: "up to 253 servos in series (with sufficient power)" ([Waveshare wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ST3215_Servo)). Practical chains: 6 per adapter in SO-101, 14 in Open Duck Mini v2.
- Connector **5264-3P**, pin 1 GND, 2 VCC, 3 Signal (item 6-7). Two sockets per servo, wired in parallel internally → straight daisy chain, no termination.
- Bus throughput (my estimate, 1 Mbps ≈ 100 kB/s): one sync-write of position+time+speed for 14 servos ≈ 106 bytes ≈ 1.1 ms; 14 individual position/speed/load reads ≈ 20–25 bytes each incl. response ≈ 0.25–0.4 ms each with servo latency → ≈ 5–8 ms → a 50–100 Hz control loop is comfortable on one chain.

### 2.5 360° / multi-turn

Yes. Feetech item 7-12/7-13: mode 3 = step mode (relative moves from the current position; set angle limits to 0 first — [Feetech FAQ item 13](https://www.feetechrc.com/Data/feetechrc/upload/file/20201127/start%20%20tutorial201015.pdf)); **multi-loop mode gives ±7 turns of absolute position at full resolution, turn count not retained at power-off, doubling turns when resolution is halved** (address 30 "angle resolution", Feetech tutorial p.5). Not needed for a leg joint, but handy for a neck/antenna or a winch.

**Can a Raspberry Pi UART drive them directly?** Yes, with a tri-state buffer. Feetech's own reference circuit (datasheet p.8 "接口定义") is: SN74LVC1G126 from host TXD to the DATA line with OE = TXEN; SN74LVC1G125 from DATA to host RXD with /OE = TXEN; a PNP transistor (10 k base, 20 k pull-down) derives TXEN from TXD so no GPIO direction control is needed; VCC = the host's logic level (3.3 V is fine, signal high spec 2–5 V). The Waveshare adapter is exactly this circuit plus a CH343P, so the cheapest robust route is the ₹450–800 adapter in UART mode wired to the Pi's GPIO14/15 UART ([pinout.xyz](https://pinout.xyz/pinout/uart)). Alternatives seen in the wild: STM32 single-wire half-duplex mode with a 500–1000 Ω pull-up ([viteo STM32 port](https://github.com/viteo/STM32-BluePill-SCServo-library)); RP2040 + Waveshare Pico driver board ([kevinmcaleer/sts3215](https://github.com/kevinmcaleer/sts3215)). Open Duck Mini v2 itself uses the Waveshare adapter **over USB** on the Pi Zero 2W with the FTDI/CH34x latency timer tuned ([Open Duck Mini Runtime, DeepWiki](https://deepwiki.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini_Runtime)).

**Power note for 14 servos:** the adapter's servo rail is marked 6 A max (schematic). 14 × 150 mA no-load = 2.1 A idle; stall on two knees alone = 5 A. Feed the servo chain(s) from the battery with your own XT30 harness (Open Duck BOM uses XT30) and pass only DATA/GND through the adapter, or split into two chains each under 6 A.

### 4.3 Import duty to India (courier / e-commerce parcel)

Stack, per [taxgst.in](https://taxgst.in/import-duty-calculator-india/): assessable value (CIF) → BCD → **SWS = 10 % of BCD** → **IGST 18 %** on (CIF + BCD + SWS). BCD for small DC motors (HS 8501.10.19) is quoted as **15 %** by [EximPe](https://eximpe.com/hsn/electric-motor-hsn-code) (their indicative total incl. an AIDC line: ₹40.39 per ₹100 CIF) and as "10–15 % for 85xx electronics" by [Alibaba's India shipping guide](https://electronics.alibaba.com/question/aliexpress-in-india-shipping,-safety-workarounds-(2025)). Arithmetic: BCD 10 % → total 31.0 % of CIF; BCD 15 % → 37.5 %; with the AIDC line EximPe shows → 40.4 %. **Budget 31–41 % of CIF plus the courier's clearance fee [fee amount unverified].** Practical constraints: the AliExpress app is delisted in India (site still usable), delivery 14–60 days, and 68–94 % customs-clearance rates depending on channel (same Alibaba guide).

### 5.1 Torque margin for a ~1.5–2 kg biped (my estimate; assumptions explicit)

Reference mass: Open Duck Mini v2's MJCF sums to **2.045 kg** (trunk 0.699, head 0.407, each leg ≈ 0.41) and it walks on **14 × STS3215 7.4 V** ([open_duck_mini_v2.xml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Playground/main/playground/open_duck_mini_v2/xmls/open_duck_mini_v2.xml), [BOM](https://orobot.io/o/program/BROKER-2/open-duck-mini)). Weight W = 2.045 × 9.81 = 20.1 N. Static single-support worst cases (1 kg·cm = 0.0981 N·m):

| Joint | Assumed lever arm (CoM offset from joint axis) | Static torque | vs C001 stall 19.5 / rated 5 | vs C018 stall 30 / rated 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ankle pitch (CoM at toe edge) | 40 mm | 0.80 N·m = 8.2 kg·cm | 2.4× / 0.61× | 3.7× / 1.2× |
| Knee (30–40° bend) | 35 mm | 0.70 N·m = 7.2 kg·cm | 2.7× / 0.70× | 4.2× / 1.4× |
| Hip roll (lateral sway) | 45 mm | 0.90 N·m = 9.2 kg·cm | 2.1× / 0.54× | 3.3× / 1.1× |

Reading: the 7.4 V unit has 2–2.7× stall margin (adequate, proven by Open Duck) but runs at 1.6–1.8× its **continuous** rating in the worst pose, which is why leg servos get warm; the 12 V C018 on 3S restores ≥ 1.1× continuous margin at identical size, weight and price (+₹195 each). STS3250 would give 5–6× stall margin but costs 2.7× and adds 20 g per joint (0.27 kg over 14 joints) — unnecessary for a 2 kg robot. STS3032 (4.5 kg·cm stall, 1.5 rated) is only viable for the head/neck/antenna joints, and it is *more* expensive in India than the STS3215. ST3215-HS trades 33 % torque for 2.4× speed; at Open Duck scale the 45–52 RPM (270–312 °/s no-load) of the STS3215 is proven sufficient, so HS is optional for a faster gait later. **Decision: STS3215 everywhere; choose C018 @ 3S unless you want to reuse the Open Duck electronics verbatim (C001 @ 2S).**

## 6. CAD/BOM checklist derived from this research

1. Model the servo from the Waveshare STEP; use the DXF numbers here only for sanity checks.
2. Mount = capture the body between printed halves; bolt loads through the horn (4 × M3 on Ø14 cross + M3×6 centre) and the idler disc; M2×6 into the 18.41 mm hole pair on each face as anti-rotation.
3. Reserve Ø22 × 3 mm for the gear-head boss, 3.4 mm spline stand-off, 4.1 mm idler stand-off, and ≥ 10 mm sideways at the rear-bottom corner for the 5264 plugs.
4. Two power chains ≤ 6 A each if the Waveshare adapter carries servo power; otherwise power via XT30 harness.
5. Set register 16/48 (torque limit) and 34–39 (overload) per joint; set 14/15 to the pack's real min/max before the first walk.
6. Order 14 + 2 spares from Evelta (24 h) and one SmartElex/Waveshare adapter from robu/Robocraze/REES52.

## Critic additions (2026-09-26, from `07_critic_gaps.md`)

1. **Mass used in §5.1 is wrong.** The Open Duck Playground MJCF sums to **2.1071 kg** (critic re-summed the 15 inertials) and the `mini_bdx` XML/URDF to **2.0615 kg**; 2.045 kg matches neither. Effect on §5.1: torques scale by 2.107/2.045 = +3 %, i.e. ankle 8.4, knee 7.4, hip roll 9.5 kg.cm static worst case — stall margins 2.05-2.6x (C001), 3.2-4.0x (C018). Conclusion unchanged. The TL;DR's "2.3-4x" should read "2.1-2.7x (C001) / 3.2-4.2x (C018)".
2. **Servo envelope as modelled in the Open Duck CAD** (critic's measurement of the reference meshes `wj-wk00-0122/0123/0124*.stl`): three shells 9.70 + 13.90 + 14.10 mm thick, **union 45.22 x 24.72 x 35.70 mm** — consistent with §1.1's 35 mm catalogue height plus the raised boss. Use 35.7 mm for the pocket height stack.
3. **Horn discs as modelled in the Open Duck CAD:** `drive_palonier` = disc **O19.95 x 5.05 mm** with O3.2 centre hole; `passive_palonier` = **O19.95 x 3.10 mm** with O6.1 bore (rides on the O6 rear idler shaft). So the reference design uses a O20 disc on both faces (Feetech's supplied aluminium discs), matching the Waveshare O19.2 / O14-cross disc in §1.4 to within 0.75 mm. Open question 2 (M3-tapped vs clearance) stays open.
4. **Connector pitch (§1.5):** Molex's 5264 SPOX family is nominally **2.50 mm** pitch, not 2.54 (Molex page timed out for the critic; treat as 2.50 mm **[unverified here]**). JST-XH / "Relimate" 2.50 mm 3-pin housings are the usual substitute but need a fit test (see 06 §7).
5. **kp mapping (open question 8):** the maintainer states "the 17.8 in mujoco corresponds to 32 in the motors firmware units" ([Open_Duck_Mini issue #20](https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini/issues/20), quoted in 02 §2.5) — i.e. ~0.556 N.m/rad per firmware P unit (critic's arithmetic; the Playground XML actually ships kp = 13.37 / 17.11, so re-identify on your leg anyway). BAM's identified parameters are in 02 §2.4 (max_velocity 5.07-5.30 rad/s, kt 1.18-1.28 N.m/A, R 2.45-2.75 ohm, armature 0.0216-0.026).
6. **robu.in price in §4.1:** the "Rs 2,097 30 kg.cm" came from a search snippet with a 403'd page; `06_electronics_bom_inr.md` read the listings the same day: Waveshare ST3215 **7.4 V Rs 2,159 (SKU R258500)**, 12 V 30 kg Rs 2,599. Trust 06, but R258500's gear ratio/voltage is still unverified (403 again for the critic).
7. **Bearing for the reference design** (relevant to §6 checklist item 2): Open Duck's passive hip-roll and head-roll pivots use **6804ZZ 20 x 32 x 7 mm** (3 pcs), not 608 — see 02 Critic additions.
